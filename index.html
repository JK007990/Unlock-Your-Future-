Our Vision

Our vision is to build an inclusive society that ensures equitable education and non-discrimination for youth, while creating meaningful and sustainable opportunities for their advancement.

Our Mission

We are dedicated to ensuring that youth have equitable access to education anytime, anywhere. We strive to provide impactful knowledge and valuable opportunities, while empowering young people to unite and share their collective skills, knowledge, and talents.

Our Goal

To enhance youth development and ensure equal educational opportunities for all children.

Academy Policies

1.Team Policy - All members must strictly refrain from any form of discrimination, whether within the team or during external operations. In working for the benefit of children and youth, any behavior or language that could harm their physical or psychological safety must be avoided. Seeking personal business advantages or profit by exploiting the organization's name and operations is absolutely prohibited. Personal matters and disputes that could damage the team's image and objectives must not be brought into internal team processes.

2.Volunteer Policy (Long-Term and Short-Term) - Long-Term Volunteers operate similarly to full-time staff. They must be individuals who can continuously collaborate with the team for a minimum duration of six (6) months or longer. They will have the opportunity to play an active role in the organization's core projects and, upon successful completion of the designated period, will be issued an official Recommendation Letter or Certificate by the organization. They must strictly adhere to the designated rules and regulations of the team. Short-Term Volunteers operate similarly to part-time staff. They must be individuals who specifically contribute to events, camps, or short-term projects (e.g., spanning from 1 week to 1 month). Upon completion of the assigned event or project, they will receive a Certificate of Appreciation for their contribution. All volunteers must deeply respect and adhere to the non-discrimination policy outlined in the Team Policy. They must execute assigned tasks within the specified timeframe with full responsibility and accountability. No volunteer is permitted to make decisions or give media interviews on behalf of the organization without official authorization.

3.Collaboration & Partnership Policy - Partner organizations collaborating with us must be entities that focus on child and youth development and embrace human rights and equality. Our organization will stand independently, ensuring it does not fall under the direction, influence, or control of any political party, business conglomerate, or religious organization. In joint collaboration projects, both parties must mutually and transparently share financial and operational reports. /p>
